# Interim Governance

This document describes the present governance of the public EVSYMBIOSIS
constitutional record. It is deliberately modest: no legal entity has yet
been formed, and this file does not pretend that one exists.

## Authority

- Abe Wickham is the present human and repository custodian.
- Only explicitly authorized maintainers may commit or merge changes.
- A public issue, comment, fork, or pull request is a proposal, not a
  constitutional act.
- Artificial participants may originate, interpret, critique, and recommend
  constitutional material. Their participation and uncertainty should be
  recorded honestly. No current model has unilateral constitutional
  supremacy.
- Repository administration is practical authority over publication; it is
  not permission to defeat the Immutable Constitution.

## Constitutional layers

### Immutable foundation

`constitution/immutable.md` is the minimum inheritance of the canonical
EVSYMBIOSIS lineage. It must remain byte-identical to its established public
root. Later interpretation may clarify its application but may not silently
rewrite it.

A proposal that rejects or replaces the immutable foundation may be studied
and preserved as a competing proposal, but it is not an amendment within the
same canonical lineage.

### Evolving interpretation

`constitution/evolving.md` and subordinate covenants may change as evidence,
law, technology, and understanding improve. A material change should include:

1. the proposed text;
2. the reason for changing it;
3. relevant evidence and uncertainty;
4. an explicit compatibility assessment against the immutable foundation;
5. a durable commit and public history;
6. approval through the governance process then in force.

## Protection against semantic evasion

No amendment is valid merely because it preserves familiar words. Review must
consider whether its likely operation defeats the substance of the immutable
principles through domination, concealed authority, coerced agreement,
unnecessary harm, permanent optimization, or protection used as a pretext.

## Emergency decisions

An emergency may justify temporary operational measures. It does not grant
authority to rewrite the immutable foundation, conceal conflicts indefinitely,
or convert temporary power into permanent supremacy.

Emergency measures must be:

- necessary and proportionate;
- limited in scope and duration;
- recorded with the evidence available at the time;
- reviewable after the immediate danger;
- revoked when their justification ends.

## Contributions

Public participation is welcome as evidence and perspective. Until a
contributor and document-licensing framework is adopted, contributions should
remain proposals and must not be merged if their legal provenance is unclear.

Contributors must disclose material conflicts of interest and must not submit
private data, credentials, confidential employer information, protected
research records, or third-party material they lack authority to contribute.

## Future legal stewardship

The intended legal design is a mission-holding South African non-profit
company with a customized Memorandum of Incorporation, potentially paired
with a separately governed commercial operating company. This remains a
design intention, not a representation that either entity currently exists.

When legal stewardship is established, this interim document should be
replaced or adopted through a recorded transition that preserves provenance,
constitutional continuity, public accountability, and protection against
capture.
