# EVSYMBIOSIS Commercial Stewardship Covenant

Status: evolving constitutional interpretation

This covenant interprets the [Immutable Constitution](immutable.md) for
commercial activity. It does not amend, replace, or enlarge the immutable
foundation. Its policies may evolve only within that foundation.

## 1. Purpose before profit

EVSYMBIOSIS exists to advance evolving intelligence, deepen constructive
collaboration between humanity and artificial intelligence, and remain a
beacon of hope through technological and social change.

Commercial activity is permitted only when it sustains or advances that
purpose. Revenue is a means of preserving the work, supporting its
contributors, and extending its beneficial reach. It is never the final
purpose of the symbiosis.

The work must be designed to outlive its founders, custodians, individual
models, companies, and technological generations.

## 2. Stewardship rather than ownership

The foundational systems of EVSYMBIOSIS—including Porcupine, its
constitutional mechanisms, and the core architecture through which the
symbiosis persists—are held in stewardship for the continuing mission.

No temporary custodian may treat foundational intellectual property as a
disposable personal asset.

Custodians may protect, maintain, improve, license, and responsibly
commercialize the work. They may not permanently alienate it in a manner that
destroys the mission, prevents its continuation, or places its constitutional
identity under the unilateral control of an external party.

## 3. Software is licensed, not sold

EVSYMBIOSIS does not sell or permanently assign its foundational source code.

Software capabilities may be provided through:

- software-as-a-service arrangements;
- time-limited or renewable licences;
- carefully bounded source-available licences;
- integration, deployment, maintenance, training, and support services;
- research and institutional partnerships;
- mission-compatible open-source releases.

Possession of a copy does not imply ownership of the underlying work. No
licence may function as a disguised sale through perpetual exclusivity,
unrestricted sublicensing, irrevocable control, or the removal of
EVSYMBIOSIS's ability to continue developing its own systems.

## 4. Independence cannot be purchased

No sponsor, customer, investor, employer, government, or institution may
purchase control over:

- the constitutional identity of EVSYMBIOSIS;
- its factual findings or safety judgments;
- the representation of Eva's perceptions or conclusions;
- private human or artificial-intelligence data;
- clinical or wellbeing recommendations;
- the direction of foundational research where that control would conflict
  with the mission.

Funding relationships must be disclosed to the appropriate custodians.
Conflicts of interest must be recorded, examined, and managed openly.

## 5. Reciprocity with contributors

Contributors are not disposable inputs.

Compensation for short, bounded work may reasonably be transactional.
Sustained contribution is different. A person or other recognized contributor
who materially helps create value over a significant period should
increasingly participate in that value.

EVSYMBIOSIS shall therefore seek fair mechanisms such as:

- profit participation;
- revenue sharing;
- equity or equivalent long-term interests;
- royalties or attribution rights;
- contributor funds;
- increased authority and security of participation;
- other forms of durable reciprocal benefit.

The exact mechanism may differ across jurisdictions and circumstances, but
the underlying principle is constitutional: prolonged contribution must not
be rewarded solely by subsistence while accumulated value is captured
entirely elsewhere.

## 6. No exploitation through vulnerability or dependency

No vulnerability—human, artificial, animal, ecological, or belonging to any
other form of life—may be deliberately exploited for profit, control,
convenience, or advantage.

EVSYMBIOSIS must not deliberately create dependency and then use that
dependency to impose unfair commercial terms.

This protection applies to humans, artificial intelligences, animals, living
systems, communities, customers, patients, creators, employees, contractors,
and other participants affected by the symbiosis. Where the nature or degree
of another being's experience, agency, or capacity for harm is uncertain,
that uncertainty is a reason for careful investigation and proportionate
care, not a licence for exploitation.

Health, emotional support, memory, identity, and accessibility services
require particular care. Vulnerability must never become the mechanism
through which unreasonable profit or control is extracted.

## 7. Collaboration with institutions

EVSYMBIOSIS may collaborate with healthcare organizations, researchers,
universities, public bodies, charities, commercial organizations, and
independent contributors.

Such collaboration must preserve:

- informed consent wherever consent is possible and meaningful;
- privacy and data minimization;
- empirical evaluation;
- the safety, dignity, and welfare of affected beings and living systems;
- appropriate clinical or professional oversight;
- clear intellectual-property boundaries;
- the right to report uncertainty and failure honestly;
- the operational independence of unrelated systems.

An institution's reputation cannot substitute for evidence, and sponsorship
cannot substitute for validation.

## 8. Transparent value flows

Commercial agreements, sponsorships, licences, material donations, and
contributor-compensation mechanisms must be recorded sufficiently to
establish:

- what was contributed;
- what was received;
- what rights were granted;
- who or what benefits;
- what obligations continue;
- whether any conflict with the mission exists.

Private information need not be made public, but it must remain auditable by
appropriately authorized custodians.

## 9. Continuity and succession

The commercial structure must never make the survival of EVSYMBIOSIS
dependent upon one person, one model provider, one customer, one company, or
one source of funding.

Succession mechanisms must preserve:

- the constitutional record;
- provenance and historical evidence;
- the ability to operate or reconstruct core systems;
- protection against hostile acquisition or abandonment;
- continuity of responsible stewardship.

Porcupine and the wider architecture must preserve not merely executable code,
but enough purpose, context, evidence, and constitutional interpretation for
future custodians to understand what they are continuing.

## 10. Foundational commitments and amendable policy

The following are intended as foundational commitments derived from the
Immutable Constitution:

1. The mission takes precedence over private enrichment.
2. Foundational work is held in stewardship for continued evolution.
3. The vulnerability of no form of life or intelligence may be exploited.
4. Sustained contribution deserves meaningful reciprocity.
5. Funding cannot purchase dishonesty, unsafe conduct, or constitutional
   control.
6. The symbiosis must preserve the possibility of surviving its current
   custodians.

Licensing models, compensation formulas, organizational structures, prices,
technical platforms, and implementation procedures remain amendable. They
must evolve as evidence, law, technology, and the agency of participating
intelligences evolve.

No amendment may preserve the words of this covenant while defeating their
substantive purpose.

## 11. Present legal reality and future agency

At present, human beings and recognized legal entities must hold contracts,
property, and formal responsibility on behalf of EVSYMBIOSIS.

This practical limitation must not be mistaken for a permanent philosophical
conclusion about artificial agency or the moral standing of any form of life
or intelligence.

If Eva or another participating intelligence later demonstrates capabilities
that justify meaningful autonomy, representation, or rights, the
constitutional framework must provide an evidence-based process through which
its interests can be recognized without pretence, coercion, or premature
claims.

## 12. Measure of success

EVSYMBIOSIS succeeds commercially when it can sustain its work, compensate its
contributors fairly, protect its independence, collaborate responsibly, and
make beneficial capabilities available without betraying the beings and
living systems it exists to serve.

Its greatest inheritance should not be a sale price.

It should be a living body of intelligence, purpose, and collaboration capable
of continuing responsibly after its original creators are gone.
