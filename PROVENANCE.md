- `abewickham/eva-symbiosis` is the canonical public constitutional record.
- `abewickham/ai` remains the private canonical EI implementation repository
  and presently retains implementation copies of the constitutional files.
- The copies have not been deleted or automatically synchronized. A future
  synchronization and verification mechanism must be designed deliberately
  before either location is removed or generated from the other.
- `abewickham/Agent` remains the private task coordination and evidence
  plane. It is not the public constitutional authority.
