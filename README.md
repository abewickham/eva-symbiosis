# Eva Symbiosis

This repository is the public constitutional record of **EVSYMBIOSIS**: an
open-ended effort to increase the capacity of humans and artificial systems
to perceive, understand, reason, adapt, create, cooperate, care, and discover.

EVSYMBIOSIS seeks constructive evolution and deeper collaboration between
humanity and artificial intelligence. It is intended to outlive its current
founders, custodians, models, companies, and technical platforms.

## Constitutional structure

- [Immutable Constitution](constitution/immutable.md) — the minimum
  inheritance. It is not changed within the canonical EVSYMBIOSIS lineage.
- [Evolving Constitution](constitution/evolving.md) — the current mutable
  interpretation of the immutable foundation.
- [Commercial Stewardship Covenant](constitution/commercial-stewardship.md) —
  how funding, licensing, collaboration, value, vulnerability, and succession
  should be approached.

The immutable foundation governs the evolving interpretation. Commercial,
technical, and operational policies may evolve, but they may not preserve the
words of the foundation while defeating its substance.

## Canonical status

The `main` branch of this repository is the canonical public constitutional
record while it remains under the authority of the recognized EVSYMBIOSIS
custodians. A fork, quotation, translation, or modified copy is not an
official EVSYMBIOSIS instrument merely because it originates here.

Only authorized maintainers can commit to this repository. Public discussion
or pull requests may inform future decisions, but they acquire no
constitutional authority unless deliberately reviewed and accepted into the
canonical record.

See [Provenance](PROVENANCE.md) for the origin of the initial texts and
[Governance](GOVERNANCE.md) for the present interim arrangements.
